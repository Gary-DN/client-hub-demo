Exposed MCP Tools & Resources

Functions your AI assistant can call in real time:

get_pipeline_deals(stage?)

Lists prospects, deal values, probability %, and PandaDoc/Xero sign-off status.

get_client_health_scores(status?)

Returns RAG health scores, overdue invoices, and 90-day upsell triggers.

get_production_profitability(client)

Compares allocated hours vs. logged hours and pending content approvals.

get_reporting_metrics(client, period)

Pulls GA4 sessions, top landing pages, Search Console keywords, and PPC ROAS.
