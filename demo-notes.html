4. Automated Slack Alert System (Dedicated Client Channels)

How Client Slack Integration Works

Every live client is linked to a dedicated Slack channel (e.g., #client-global-dynamics) during Onboarding. ClientHub360 automatically fires real-time alerts to that channel whenever key events happen:

  • Tasks Marked Complete: Notifies the account team (and optionally the client channel) when deliverables move to Done.
  • Connected Accounts Disconnecting / Expiring: Instant warning if GA4, Search Console, Google Ads, or Meta Business Manager loses OAuth access or a token is about to expire.
  • Content Approvals & Proposal Sign-Off: Alerts when a client clicks Yes (Approve), No (Revise), or signs an SOW.
  • Budget Pacing & Health Score Drops: Flags ad spend anomalies, overdue invoices, or a client dropping to AMBER / RED.

5. Google Meet Scheduling, Attendance Tracking & Auto-Saved Call Notes

Inside Production → Meetings, Account Managers can schedule Google Meet calls directly on a client's project page:

  • In-App Scheduling (Google Calendar API): Creates the calendar invite, emails the client, and generates the meet.google.com/... link inside ClientHub360.
  • Attendance Verification (Google Meet REST API): Checks conferenceRecords & participants after the call to automatically log Completed (duration + who joined) or flag a Client No-Show (which can feed into the Client Health Score).
  • Auto-Saved Call Notes (Meet Transcripts + Drive API): When Gemini "Take notes for me" or transcripts are enabled on Google Workspace, the Hub pulls the summary & action items and saves them automatically into the client's Meetings log and Files tab.

6. Spec Note: SEO Budget vs. Content Tasks & Upsells

Production Build Rule:

  • SEO Campaign Budget Includes Content Tasks: An SEO campaign has a single commercial SEO value/budget, which covers the Content team's included deliverables (e.g., monthly blogs & on-page copy). Those tasks appear on the Content board for delivery & client approval, while drawing their allocated hours/profitability from the SEO budget.
  • Standalone Content Upsell Projects: Separately, the Content team can also receive dedicated upsell projects (e.g., resource guides/ebooks, extra blog packages, or copy rewrites) which carry their own separate Content budget alongside the SEO retainer.
